Microsoft Says Services Fully Restored After Day-Long Global Outage

📅 BBC News Report • ⏱️ 2 Min Read Aloud • bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6rrg51rkz09o
Listen first, then read aloud to calibrate your accent.

Microsoft has officially announced that its core cloud and enterprise services have been fully restored following an unprecedented worldwide outage that disrupted Outlook, Microsoft Teams, and Copilot for hundreds of millions of users across more than seventy countries.

According to Microsoft engineering leaders, the disruption was triggered during a routine network capacity upgrade rather than a malicious cyber-attack. Engineering teams worked around the clock to deploy resilient architecture safeguards, ensuring that enterprise workloads and cloud APIs recovered safely. This global incident highlights the critical trajectory of modern cloud engineering, where automated failover systems and fault tolerance determine business continuity.
